## PROCESS OBSERVATIONS

1. **The stranded-fix ledger is now the repo's biggest risk.** Three branches
   hold overlapping copies of the same fixes (money-path-landing,
   redteam-backlog-sweep, plus the speed branch's independent inference.py
   lineage), none merged, while master accumulates red canaries. Next merge
   of ANY of these will conflict heavily in tools/kelly.py, tools/edge.py,
   tools/pipeline/engine.py — exactly the conditions that produced the pm1
   revert. Recommendation: land ONE lineage (backlog-sweep, the most
   complete) into master in a single supervised merge with the full suite as
   the gate, then rebase the others onto it the same day.
2. **Reports should carry suite-wide status, not scoped status.** "53/53
   green" from the speed agent and "22/28 fixed" from the sweep are both true
   and together hide "master is 53-red". A one-line `SUITE: N failed on this
   head` field in every findings file would have surfaced defect B
   immediately.
3. Mutation testing worked as advertised and cheaply (~30 min for 5
   mutations). Recommend adding it to the standing review loop per run.
