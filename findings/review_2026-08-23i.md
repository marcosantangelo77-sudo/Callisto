## WHAT DOES NOT HOLD UP

### RV11-A (MEDIUM) — source-health's USPTO probe reads absence as success (family #3, inside the anti-family-#3 module)

health.py's stated reason to exist: *"HTTP 200 but ZERO results … is a
FAILURE, not a pass; it is precisely how the ClinicalTrials and FDIC defects
hid."* Its own USPTO count function defeats that:

```python
lambda d: int((d.get("count") or d.get("total") or 1))
```

A payload `{"total": 0}` → row_count **1** → `_finish` never sees zero →
shape check passes ("total" present) → verdict **OK**, evidence literally
"1 rows, shape as expected". DEGRADED is unreachable for this source via
zero results. Verified by driving the real registered thunk
(`H.PROBES["uspto_odp"][1]()`) over a stub transport. Same `or 1` shape also
maps missing-count payloads to a positive count. Blast radius: the probe
reports green through exactly the drift it was built to catch.

Repro: `test_uspto_probe_zero_results_is_degraded_not_ok`,
`test_health_count_helpers_cannot_map_zero_to_positive`.

Related note, same module: `run_all()` has zero callers outside its own
`__main__`. Opt-in CLI is the documented design, but nothing schedules it —
no cron/watchdog/API endpoint — so the five historical live-API defects it
exists to catch recur until someone remembers to type the command. A health
monitor nobody runs is W5's signature check with extra steps.
