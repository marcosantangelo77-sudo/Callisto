## What does not — ranked

### RT1 (HIGH) — the money-path redteam ships two repros pinned to WRONG inputs, and one that passes vacuously (family 7 inside a family-hunting suite)

The suite's headline defect M1 is described as "yes_ask=0.60 with no_ask=
0.61, overround −0.19". That arithmetic is false: 0.60 + 0.61 sums to 1.21,
i.e. overround **+0.21** — a wide-but-valid-looking book, NOT a crossed one.
Consequences, measured on 9324806:

- `test_m1_crossed_book_overround_negative_must_be_rejected` FAILS at the
  wrong assert, reporting overround=0.21 — it demonstrates only "devig has
  no sanity gate", which is M1c's stated subject, not M1's.
- `test_m1b_stale_snapshot_mix_manufactures_actionable_edge` dies at its own
  PRECONDITION (`assert a.devig_audit["overround"] < 0` → 0.21 < 0 fails).
  It never reaches the invariant it exists to prove.
- `test_m1c_assess_edge_has_no_overround_sanity_gate` PASSES VACUOUSLY: all
  three chosen pairs land in (0, 0.5) — [(0.60,0.61)→0.21, (0.55,0.50)→0.05,
  (0.30,0.95)→0.25] — so its `assert 0 < over < 0.5` can never fire. A gate
  "tested" only with inputs the gate would accept even if it existed.

The underlying defect is real (I reproduce it properly with genuinely
negative overround: price 0.45 / counter 0.50 → overround −0.05 → fair
0.474, actionable=True, Kelly 0.25 — see RT-repros in
tests/test_review_2026-08-23f.py), and the findings file's fix direction is
right. But 3 of 7 repros do not pin the defect they name. Per PATTERNS #7:
a demonstration whose inputs never touch the failing boundary manufactures
confidence — in the very artifact whose job is to destroy it. The merge
decision for this branch should rest on the corrected repros, and the
findings file's "overround −0.19" narrative needs correcting before anyone
implements against it.
