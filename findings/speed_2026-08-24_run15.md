## THE MECHANISM — why the fork fallback is not a fallback

Failover is SEQUENTIAL: proxy patience runs to exhaustion FIRST (~35s),
THEN the fork starts and queues on the SAME Portal capacity. Both tiers
share one upstream; the fork buys no alternative queue, only ~12–14s of
process startup on top of the identical wait. Tonight's forks took 48–78s
wall ON TOP of the 35s already spent waiting in place.

Two defects compound:

1. **`attempts=2` binds before the patience budget does.** With attempts=2
   at most ONE Retry-After window can ever be honoured: attempt 1 sleeps
   RA:30, attempt 2 gets another 429 and the budget check (30+30 > 35)
   raises. `_429_PATIENCE_S` could be 1000s and two stacked windows would
   still fail over. Portal's observed pattern under load IS consecutive
   ~30s windows (run 10's raw-curl control measured stacks of 60–128s).
2. **Rate-limit exhaustion poisons endpoint health.** Budget exhaustion
   raises a generic HTTPStatusError → `complete()` treats it like any
   failure → `record_failure()` ratchets the HEALTHY proxy's exponential
   cooldown 2s→4s→…→60s for an upstream-capacity condition shared by
   every tier in the pool. Sustained pressure locks later calls out of
   the fast path entirely — they skip the cooldown-blocked proxy and pay
   full fork cost even after Portal recovers.
